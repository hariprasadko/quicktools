Free browser-based tools for developers, images and everyday finance

Quicktools is a small collection of 22 focused utilities — a JSON formatter, JWT decoder, screenshot stitcher, color palette extractor, image-to-PDF converter, and stock, loan and interest calculators. No sign-up, no installs, no ads.

Every tool runs entirely in your browser — your files and data are never uploaded anywhere.
